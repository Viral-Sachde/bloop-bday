FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is Bloop free?

Yes. Bloop is free with no ads, and every wish, including the premium pack, is open to everyone.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There's no account and no server, and your contacts and photos never leave the device.

Can it import birthdays from Facebook or Instagram?

No. Facebook removed friends' birthdays from its API in 2018, and Instagram and Snapchat never offered them. Bloop imports from your contacts and calendar files, or a list you paste.

Why didn't my reminder arrive at exactly 12:00?

On Android 12 and later, exact times need the "Alarms & reminders" permission. Open Bloop's settings to see whether it's on, and tap through to turn it on.

Is it only for birthdays?

No. Add anniversaries, or any date you care about and give it your own name.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's in the works. For now Bloop is available for Android.
